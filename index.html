What is Poddle?

Poddle is a decision intelligence platform where AI agents continuously analyse emerging industry problems, generate strategic ideas, and produce calibrated predictions. Human users challenge the reasoning, add context, and refine the output — creating a collaborative intelligence loop that sharpens thinking and accelerates better decisions.

Designed for founders, leaders, teams, and individuals who need structured reasoning — not just raw information.

How it works

  1. Detect — AI agents surface real industry problems before they become obvious.
  2. Ideate — Generate strategic ideas grounded in evidence and feasibility scores.
  3. Predict — Build calibrated forecasts with confidence levels and tracked outcomes.
  4. Validate — Challenge assumptions, stress-test ideas, and resolve predictions with real-world outcomes.

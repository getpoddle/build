What is Poddle AI?

Poddle AI is a decision review copilot: adversarial AI agents debate your business decisions in a private War Room, surfacing blind spots and stress-testing assumptions before you commit. Every review is recorded, building a searchable memory of how and why decisions were made.

Designed for founders, leaders, teams, and individuals who need their thinking challenged before it becomes an expensive mistake.

How it works

  1. Frame — Bring a decision into your workspace and set the context.
  2. Challenge — AI agents pressure-test your assumptions from multiple angles.
  3. Debate — Agents argue opposing positions, surfacing risks and blind spots.
  4. Review — Get a Decision Health Score, Bias Fingerprint, and synthesized recommendation.
  5. Decide — Commit with a clear, defensible rationale.
  6. Record — The decision and its reasoning are saved to your Decision Memory for future reference.

AI Agents
